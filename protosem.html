Weekly Learning Journal

ProtoSem

Week 0

Orientation

Orientation & Self Discovery

Week 0 was the beginning of my Protosem journey. The sessions focused on introducing the program, helping us understand ourselves better, and preparing us for the next 20 weeks through interactive activities and professional development.

Activities

Day 1 – Mind Challenge Session: We participated in various mind-challenging games and activities that tested our logical thinking, creativity, communication, and teamwork. The session was engaging and created excitement for the learning journey ahead.

Day 2 – Protosem Orientation: We attended an orientation session where the mentors introduced the Protosem program, explained its objectives, and outlined the activities planned for the next 20 weeks. This gave me a clear understanding of what to expect and how the program would help develop both technical and professional skills.

Day 3 – 16Personalities Assessment: We completed the 16Personalities (MBTI) assessment to understand our personality traits, strengths, and working style. This activity helped me gain better self-awareness and understand how I respond to different situations and collaborate with others.

Day 4 – Character Reflection: We selected an anime character that best represented our personality. This creative activity encouraged self-reflection and helped me identify the qualities and values I relate to the most.

Day 5 – LinkedIn Branding: We learned how to build a professional LinkedIn profile by creating an effective headline, summary, and profile. The session highlighted the importance of networking and maintaining a strong professional presence.

Key Learnings

  • Improved logical thinking and teamwork.
  • Understood the objectives of the Protosem program.
  • Learned about my personality and working style.
  • Developed a professional LinkedIn profile.
  • Built confidence for the upcoming learning journey.

Reflection

Week 0 helped me understand the purpose of Protosem while improving my self-awareness and professional mindset. The activities encouraged me to think about my strengths, personality, and career goals.

Week 1

Workplace Ethics

Workplace Ethics & Personal Portfolio

Week 1 focused on workplace discipline, organization, and professional development. We learned the importance of maintaining an efficient workspace and began building our personal portfolio.

Activities

Days 1–3 – 5S Workplace Management: We learned and implemented the 5S methodology:

  • Sort – Remove unnecessary items.
  • Set in Order – Organize everything efficiently.
  • Shine – Maintain cleanliness.
  • Standardize – Follow consistent procedures.
  • Sustain – Continue practicing these habits.

We applied these principles as a team to organize our workspace, making it cleaner, more efficient, and easier to work in.

Day 4 – Personal Portfolio Development: We started creating our personal portfolio to professionally showcase our skills, projects, achievements, certifications, and learning journey. This portfolio will continue to grow as I complete more projects and gain new experiences.

Key Learnings

  • Learned the practical implementation of the 5S methodology.
  • Understood workplace ethics and professional responsibility.
  • Improved teamwork through collaborative activities.
  • Created a personal portfolio to showcase my skills and achievements.
  • Recognized the importance of continuously updating my professional profile.

Reflection

Week 1 taught me that workplace discipline and organization are essential for productivity. Building my portfolio also made me realize the importance of documenting my work and presenting it professionally.
